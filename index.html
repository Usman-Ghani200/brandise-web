FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How long does a typical project take?

Brand design and video editing packages take 1–3 weeks, web applications & custom sites take 3–6 weeks, and AI automations take 1–3 weeks. You will receive an exact timeline in your proposal.

Can I combine multiple services like Video Editing and Social Media?

Yes, you can combine services, such as Video Editing with Social Media Management or Web App Development with AI Automation.

How many revision rounds are included?

Every project includes 2 to 3 consolidated revision rounds to ensure the final deliverable matches your vision.

What are your payment terms?

Projects begin with a 50% deposit in USD, with the balance due upon project delivery and final approval. Retainer services are billed monthly in advance.

Do you work with clients in the US and worldwide?

Yes! We work with US, European, and global clients via Slack, Zoom, Email, and WhatsApp.

How do we get started?
