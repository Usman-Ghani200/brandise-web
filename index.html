FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a project take?

Most branding projects take 2–6 weeks, websites 3–6 weeks, and automations 1–4 weeks. You'll get an exact timeline in your proposal.

How many revisions are included?

Each package includes 2–3 consolidated revision rounds. Extra rounds are quoted at our standard rate.

What are your payment terms?

Projects start with a 50% deposit in USD and the balance on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance. We accept bank transfer and major online payment platforms.

What do I need to provide?

Your goals, any existing brand assets, access to relevant accounts and timely feedback. We'll send a simple checklist after the first call.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We're based in Karachi, Pakistan and work with US clients remotely over video, email and WhatsApp.

Do you offer monthly retainers?

Yes. Marketing, content and automation support are available monthly with 30 days' notice to cancel.
