1. Who we are

Brandise ("Brandise", "we", "us") provides design, marketing and AI automation services to businesses in the United States and elsewhere. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and in the course of our work. It is written for US visitors and clients, including residents of California and other states with privacy laws.

Contact: hello.brandise@gmail.com · [YOUR BUSINESS ADDRESS]

2. Information we collect

In the past 12 months we have collected these categories of personal information, which may include:

  • Identifiers: name, business name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, IP address.
  • Commercial information: the services you enquire about, proposals, invoices and payment history (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Business and project data: briefs, brand assets, account access and files you choose to share.
  • Internet activity: pages viewed, browser and device type, and referring page, from our hosting provider and, if enabled, analytics tools.
  • Communications: messages you send by form, email, WhatsApp or social media.

We collect this directly from you, from your use of the site, and from service providers acting for us. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (such as Social Security numbers, health or precise location data). Please do not send us any.

3. How we use it

  • Responding to enquiries and preparing quotes.
  • Delivering, managing and supporting our services.
  • Invoicing, payments, accounting and tax records.
  • Sending service updates and, where permitted, marketing emails.
  • Securing and improving the website, and preventing spam and fraud.
  • Complying with law and enforcing our agreements.

4. Who we share it with

We disclose personal information for business purposes to service providers that process it on our behalf, such as website hosting (GitHub Pages), form delivery, email and messaging tools (for example Gmail and WhatsApp), file-sharing and project tools, payment and invoicing providers, and accountants or lawyers. We may also disclose information to comply with law, respond to legal process, or protect rights and safety, and in connection with a business sale or merger. Service providers may use data only to perform services for us.

When we use AI tools on client projects, we do not put confidential client materials into them without your agreement.

5. We do not sell your information

In the past 12 months we have not sold personal information for money, and we have not shared it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16. If we later enable advertising pixels (such as Meta Pixel) or similar tools that could count as "sharing", we will update this policy first, add a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link, and honor opt-outs.

6. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose and sell or share, and request a copy;
  • Delete personal information we collected from you, with legal exceptions;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information;
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information (we do not use it beyond what is permitted); and
  • Non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

California's "Shine the Light" law also lets residents ask which personal information we disclosed to third parties for their direct marketing. We do not do this. Business-to-business contact information is covered by this policy where it identifies you as an individual.

7. Other US state rights

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws (including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and others) may have rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a portable copy of their data, and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale and certain profiling. If we deny a request you may appeal by replying to our decision email or writing to us with "Appeal" in the subject line. If your appeal is denied you may contact your state attorney general. Nevada residents may submit an opt-out request for the sale of covered information using the contact below; we do not sell it.

8. How to make a request

Email hello.brandise@gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject and tell us which right you want to use. We will verify your identity by matching details you give us with what we hold, and may ask for more information. We respond within 45 days, and may extend once by another 45 days where permitted, with notice. You may use an authorized agent; we may require written proof of authority. Requests are free unless excessive or repetitive.

9. Cookies, Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track

This site does not currently set its own cookies. If we enable analytics or advertising tools, those providers may set cookies or similar technologies. We treat a browser's Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as a valid request to opt out of sale and sharing. Because there is no common standard, we do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

10. Email and text messages

We send marketing emails only in line with the CAN-SPAM Act: clear sender details, our postal address, no misleading subject lines, and a working unsubscribe link that we honor within 10 business days. We do not send marketing text messages or calls without your prior express written consent under the TCPA. Service messages about your project are not marketing.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA) or sell the data of anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Retention and security

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond (generally up to 24 months without a project) and client records for as long as needed for support, disputes, and tax and legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards such as access controls and encrypted connections, but no system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information we will notify you and regulators as required by applicable US state laws.

13. Where data is processed

Brandise operates from Pakistan, so personal information of US users is transferred to and processed in Pakistan, the United States and other countries where our providers operate. By using the site or our services you understand your information may be processed outside your state or country.

14. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Material changes will be posted on this page.
