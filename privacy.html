1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name, email address, and contact details you provide via our contact form or by emailing us directly
  • Your business name, phone or WhatsApp number, and service inquiry details when submitted through our website
  • Project-related information shared by clients during the engagement
  • Communication data (emails, messages, feedback)

We do not collect payment card details, government identification, or sensitive personal data through our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and manage our services
  • Communicate with you projects and updates
  • Improve our services and client experience

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Protection

We take appropriate measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services that may process personal information submitted through our website:

  • Formspree — We use Formspree to process contact form submissions. When you submit the contact form on our website, your name, email address, and message are transmitted to Formspree's servers for delivery to our business inbox. Formspree is subject to its own Privacy Policy. We do not control Formspree's data retention practices.
  • Google Fonts — Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address per Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use these services to sell or share your personal data for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies & Tracking

If you visit our website, basic cookies may be used to improve user experience and analyze traffic.

6. Data Sharing

We only share your information:

  • When required by law
  • When necessary to deliver services (e.g., collaboration tools)

7. Your Rights
